# Rain: App Store and legal readiness

## Decision that controls launch

**Rain should not ship with Buienradar’s feeds unless Buienradar gives written permission covering this watch app, its free or paid distribution, the radar imagery, and the use of its name and branding.** This is verified: Buienradar says its freely available weather data is for non-commercial use only, requires attribution, and that mobile-app or commercial use requires permission. A public endpoint and a key copied from its website do not grant a licence. No written permission is present in the repo. (Buienradar, [Free weather data](https://www.buienradar.nl/overbuienradar/gratis-weerdata); Apple, [Content rights](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#intellectual-property))

“Commercial” is not limited to charging for Rain: App Store distribution is a mobile application, for which Buienradar expressly requires permission. Paid distribution makes the commercial restriction independently relevant. Permission should explicitly cover derived rain graph values, radar tiles/frames and their recolouring/cropping, caching, redistribution in widgets, commercial App Store distribution, required attribution wording/placement, API key use and trademark/name use. This is a prudent scope inferred from the app’s use and the permission page; Buienradar’s exact grant terms cannot be known without a grant.

## App Store Connect launch requirements

- **Developer membership:** paid Apple Developer Program membership is required for App Store distribution. Team ID `7M8BR7R488` in the project is evidence of configured team signing, not proof that membership is active or agreements are current. Verify membership/agreements in the account when authorised; no sign-in was performed. [Apple Developer Program](https://developer.apple.com/programs/).
- **App record:** create through App Store Connect’s iOS app-record flow, then configure watchOS. Watch-only apps do not need iPhone screenshots. Provide app description, support URL, privacy policy URL, age rating, copyright, review contact and review notes. [Watch metadata](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-watchos-app-information/).
- **Privacy policy/App Privacy:** required policy URL and App Privacy declarations are submission requirements. Include third-party providers. The repo has no policy or manifest. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy/).
- **Privacy manifest:** perform the dependency/API audit described above. Not a blanket requirement solely because this is an app; it depends on covered APIs and SDKs. [Apple manifest docs](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/privacy_manifest_files).
- **Export compliance:** answer encryption questions for each build. HTTPS uses platform encryption, so do not assume “no encryption”; determine Apple’s applicable exemption and set the Info.plist export-compliance key if eligible. No custom cryptography is evident. [Apple export compliance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/overview-of-export-compliance).
- **Age rating:** complete the current App Store Connect questionnaire; rating is required. A weather utility with no mature content will likely receive the lowest suitable rating, but the questionnaire determines it. Do not select Kids category unless Josh intends its lasting rules. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/set-an-app-age-rating).
- **EU DSA trader status:** the developer must self-assess whether he acts as a trader in this app’s commercial activity. Being an individual does not settle it; offering a paid app or carrying on a professional/business activity points towards trader status. Apple says trader contact details (address, phone, email) are verified and displayed on EU product pages; a non-trader declaration means consumers are told statutory consumer rights do not apply to contracts with them. Decide status before submission and consider the public-address consequence. Apple’s workflow may require documentation. [Apple DSA requirements](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-compliance-information/manage-european-union-digital-services-act-trader-requirements/); [DSA Article 30](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R2065). Whether Rain’s actual setup makes Josh a trader is a legal fact-specific conclusion, not verified here.
- **Price and tax:** free apps need a price set to free; paid apps require the Account Holder to accept the Paid Apps Agreement, set a price, and enter banking and tax information. Apple requires US tax forms from all developers under that agreement; other forms depend on country. [Price](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-pricing/set-a-price/), [workflow](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/get-started/app-store-connect-workflow/), [tax](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-tax-information/provide-tax-information/).
- **Icon:** current watchOS icon specification is a layered 1088 × 1088 px square source, system-masked to a circle. A watch asset is not shown in the repo inventory; add the correct icon asset before archive. [Apple HIG icon specs](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons).
- **Screenshots:** Apple Watch screenshots are required: one to ten PNG/JPEG images, with an accepted size. For Ultra 3 the current size is **422 × 514 px**; other supported sizes are 410×502 (Ultra 2/Ultra), 416×496 (Series 12/11/10), 396×484 (Series 9/8/7), 368×448 (Series 6/5/4 and SE 3/2/SE), or 312×390 (Series 3/2/1). Use one size consistently across localisations. [Apple screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/).

## Name: “Rain”

Apple permits names from 2–30 characters, so “Rain” passes the technical length rule. That does not reserve the name or establish trademark clearance. App Store Connect name availability must be checked when creating the record; no account was accessed. A short common English word is likely crowded and hard to search, and may conflict with prior marks in weather/software classes. **Trademark status and availability are unverified.** Search EUIPO/TMview, BOIP and relevant national registers, plus App Store storefronts, before committing; check the exact word and close variants for software/weather services. Keep “Buienradar” out of the product name absent written permission. [Apple name limits](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/); [EUIPO search](https://euipo.europa.eu/); [BOIP register](https://www.boip.int/en/trademarks-register).
